# Sales Data Analysis Using Python

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **CodeOrbit Tech Data Analyst Internship**.

The objective was to analyze sales data for 2024 using Python and Pandas, identify meaningful business trends, and present the findings through data visualizations and a summary report.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Python 3.14.7** for data analysis
- **Pandas** for data manipulation, grouping, and aggregation
- **Matplotlib** for data visualization
- **Microsoft Excel** for data cleaning

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains sales transaction records from January to November 2024.

- Total Orders: 55
- Total Columns: 14
- Product Categories: Electronics, Furniture, Clothing
- Regions: North, South, East, West
- Payment Methods: UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash

## Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed using Pandas:

1. **Sales Metrics:** Calculated total sales, total orders, total quantity sold, and average order value.
2. **Product Analysis:** Identified the top-selling products based on sales.
3. **Regional Analysis:** Compared sales across different regions.
4. **Category Analysis:** Examined the contribution of each product category to total sales.
5. **Monthly Trend Analysis:** Analyzed sales performance across months.
6. **Payment Method Analysis:** Compared sales across different payment methods.
7. **Salesperson and Customer Analysis:** Identified the highest-performing salesperson and top customer.
8. **Order Status Analysis:** Examined order statuses, including cancellations.

## Key Findings

- **Total Sales:** ₹27,65,230
- **Total Orders:** 55
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 287
- **Average Order Value:** Approximately ₹50,277
- **Highest-Selling Region:** North
- **Leading Product Category:** Electronics, contributing approximately 73.9% of sales
- **Top Payment Method:** UPI
- **Top Product:** TV
- **Top Salesperson:** Amit
- **Top Customer:** Rahul Sharma
- **Peak Sales Month:** June 2024
- **Lowest Sales Month:** February 2024

## Visualizations

Four visualizations were created using Matplotlib:

1. **Sales by Region:** A bar chart comparing sales across regions.
2. **Category Share:** A pie chart showing each product category's share of sales.
3. **Monthly Sales Trend:** A line chart illustrating monthly sales variations.
4. **Sales by Payment Method:** A bar chart comparing sales across payment methods.

## Business Insights and Recommendations

- Review the strong sales performance of the North region to identify practices that may be useful elsewhere.
- Monitor Electronics inventory and demand to support the leading product category.
- Evaluate UPI usage and its associated transaction costs before deciding on payment-related strategies.
- Investigate cancelled orders to understand their impact on sales.
- Explore strategies to improve performance during lower-sales months, particularly February and August.
